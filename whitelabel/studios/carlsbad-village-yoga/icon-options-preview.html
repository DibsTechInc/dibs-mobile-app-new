Recommendation: Option B

  • The wordmark is unreadable at 60px. In Option A “CARLSBAD VILLAGE YOGA” renders around 4px tall — it reads as a grey smudge under the badge and makes the whole icon feel cluttered.
  • The app name already appears under the icon. iOS prints “CV Yoga” right there, so the wordmark is repeating information at the cost of the mark's size.
  • Option B makes the badge ~40% bigger in the same tile, so the figure and the sun read at a glance.
  • Caveat, honestly: Option B upscales 1.61× from the 774px source, so its edges are slightly soft under magnification. Invisible at 60px, faintly visible on the 1024 App Store listing. A fresh 1024² export of the badge from the .eps would remove even that — worth two minutes if the designer is available.
