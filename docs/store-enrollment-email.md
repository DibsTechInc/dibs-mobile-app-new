**Subject:** Setting up [Studio Name]'s app — two accounts to create (about 30 minutes)

Hi [First name],

We're about to start building [Studio Name]'s own iOS and Android app. One piece has to come from you: Apple and Google both require branded apps to be published under the studio's own developer account — the app appears in the stores under your name, which is exactly what we want. You set the accounts up once; we handle everything after that (building, submitting, and dealing with app review).

Here's exactly what to do.

**Apple — about 20 minutes, plus Apple's verification**

1. Check whether your business already has a D-U-N-S number: https://developer.apple.com/enroll/duns-lookup/ — many LLCs do. If not, request one from that same page. It's free and usually takes 1–2 weeks, which is why this is worth starting now.
2. Enroll in the Apple Developer Program as an **Organization** at https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/ ($99/year). Use a business email for the Apple ID, and enroll as the person with authority to sign for the business. Apple occasionally calls to confirm details — that's normal.
3. Once approved, sign in at https://appstoreconnect.apple.com → Users and Access → add **developer@ondibs.com** with the **Admin** role.

(If the studio isn't a registered legal entity, you'd enroll as an Individual instead — no D-U-N-S needed. Tell me and I'll send those steps.)

**Google — about 15 minutes**

1. Create a Play Console developer account at https://play.google.com/console/signup ($25, one-time). Choose the **Organization** account type — the same D-U-N-S number works here.
2. Once Google verifies the account: Users and permissions → invite **developer@ondibs.com** as an **Admin**.

That's everything. The D-U-N-S wait is the only slow part, so starting this week keeps the app on schedule. Reply when both are done — or if anything is confusing, and we'll get on a quick call and click through it together.

[Alicia]
